AT A GLANCE

Grounded retrieval

I designed and built the platform as the sole engineer, connecting document ingestion to evidence-backed assessments and human review.

Ask about this work ↗

THE PROBLEM

What needed solving.

Heterogeneous requests and regulatory documents need to become structured requirements that reviewers can trace back to supporting evidence.

THE APPROACH

How the pieces connect.

The pipeline combines document parsing, LLM extraction, hybrid retrieval, reranking, provenance checks and structured judgments. Human reviewers remain part of the decision flow.

CONTRIBUTION

My part in the work.

I owned backend APIs, evaluation, deployment and iterative redesign informed by customer demos and feedback.

OUTCOME

What came out of it.

A platform spanning document, spreadsheet, email and image evidence.
