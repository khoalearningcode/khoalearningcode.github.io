In a two-person team, I owned backend and infrastructure implementation for a customer-service automation system used in internal operations.

Ask about this work ↗

THE PROBLEM

What needed solving.

Support teams need fast answers to repeat questions and a reliable handoff when the system cannot answer.

THE APPROACH

How the pieces connect.

Zalo OA webhooks connect to server-hosted n8n workflows for retrieval, response generation and replies. Redis supports cache, queues and session state. Unsupported questions go to staff, and verified responses feed back into the knowledge flow.

CONTRIBUTION

My part in the work.

I built and operated backend and infrastructure components using Linux, Docker/Nginx, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Qdrant, Redis and Ollama.

OUTCOME

What came out of it.

Approximately 1,000 messages per day, with about 90% of requests handled automatically in internal CS operations.

CONTEXT & LIMITATIONS

Operational figures are approximate and reported in my CV. No customer conversations or internal datasets are shared.
