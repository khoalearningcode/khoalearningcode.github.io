EYECODE TECH · JUN — AUG 2026

Less computation.
Measured in practice.

Efficient Vision-Language-Action inference for SmolVLA.

PyTorch · SmolVLA · LeRobot/SO100 · LIBERO-10

MY ROLE

AI Researcher Intern

Token pruning, transformer layer bypass, runtime telemetry and reproducible efficiency evaluation.

CONTEXT

Compute savings need a real-world check.

Reducing a model’s compute does not automatically give the same reduction in latency. The work examined compute, rollout latency and task quality together.

APPROACH

Change the actual forward path.

I implemented forward-path token pruning and layer bypass, with telemetry and ablation pipelines. The work later moved toward trainable context-conditioned token and layer routing.

RESULTS · SEPARATE EVALUATIONS

Two observations, kept distinct.

A joint step-aware ablation reduced normalized compute from 1.00 to approximately 0.45, while rollout latency changed from 344 ms to 317 ms.

In a separate LIBERO-10 evaluation, reducing flow-matching steps from 10 to 5 reduced latency from 236.97 ms to 170.03 ms (28.25%), with aggregate task performance maintained over 50 episodes.

CONTEXT & LIMITATIONS

These values are reported in my CV and refer to specific evaluation settings. They are not a claim of universal speedup or unchanged performance on all tasks.
