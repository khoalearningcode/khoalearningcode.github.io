THE IDEA

Use the VLM’s reasoning.
Keep the detector lightweight.

A monocular detector must infer 3D geometry from a single image. MonoLaDN uses a frozen vision-language model to generate geometric reasoning during training, then removes that reasoning path at inference.

MY CONTRIBUTION · 3D REASONING GENERATION

Start with the object.
Give geometry a language.

I owned VLM-based 3D reasoning generation. The stage combines object-centric visual inputs with geometry-to-language mapping, producing structured reasoning for occlusion, depth, dimension and angle.
